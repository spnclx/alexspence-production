Privacy policy

Last updated 12 November 2026

This page explains what information is collected when you visit alexspenceproduction.com, why, and the choices you have. In short: I collect what you send me through the contact form, plus basic visitor statistics. I never sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who I am

This website is run by Alex Spence Production LLC, a video and photo production company based in Denver, Colorado, USA. For any question about this policy or your information, email alexspenceproduction@gmail.com.

Contact form

When you send a message through the contact form, I receive the details you enter: your name, email address, project type, budget and message. The form is delivered by Formspree, which passes your message on to my email inbox.

I use these details only to reply to you and to discuss your project. I keep the messages in my email as part of normal business records, and I will delete them if you ask.

Visitor statistics

Cloudflare Web Analytics

I use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. It does not use cookies, does not track you across other websites and does not build a profile of you. It records things like which pages were viewed, the site that sent you here, your browser and device type, your country and how fast the page loaded. I only see totals, never individual visitors.

Google Analytics

If you click Accept on the cookie notice, the site also loads Google Analytics. It sets cookies in your browser so it can tell new visits from returning ones, and it records the pages you view, roughly where you are, your device and browser, and how you found the site. If you click Decline, or never answer, Google Analytics does not load and no analytics cookies are set.

Google handles this data under the Google privacy policy. You can read how Google uses data from sites that use its services, or block Google Analytics on every site with the Google Analytics opt out browser add on.

Changing your choice: your answer is saved in your browser. To be asked again, clear this site's cookies and site data in your browser settings, then reload the page.

Other services the site uses

  • Videos. Portfolio videos are hosted on YouTube. Preview images load from YouTube when you open the page, and the player loads from youtube-nocookie.com only when you press play. Once a video plays, YouTube may collect information under the Google privacy policy.
  • Fonts. The site's fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address so it can send the font files.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and its domain's DNS is managed by Cloudflare. Like any web service, they may keep short term server logs, including IP addresses, for security and reliability.
  • Links to other sites. Links to Instagram, my print shop and other sites take you to services with their own privacy policies.

What I don't do

  • I don't sell or rent your personal information.
  • I don't use advertising cookies or show ads.
  • I don't share your information with anyone except the services listed on this page, or when the law requires it.

Your choices

You can ask me to show you, correct or delete any information I hold about you, such as messages you sent through the contact form. Email alexspenceproduction@gmail.com and I'll reply within 30 days.

This site is not aimed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If I change how the site handles information, I'll update this page and the date at the top.
